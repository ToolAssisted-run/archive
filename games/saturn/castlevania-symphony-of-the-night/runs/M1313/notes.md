!! Out Of Boundary Glitch
A video recap of the technique can be found [http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7ltTYChFa0E|here]
%%%
%%%For alucard and maria, its quite simple, what you want to do is to use backdash near the target door (keep or inverted chapel), push forward the direction your character is facing while backdash and repeat until you "touch" the door, keep pressing direction and youll "warp"
%%%
%%%For richter its a bit more complicated, the only way to get the same "effect" is to use the airdash, at the end of airdash you got a few frames where you can "extra" jump, if you land on door exactly on the frame your given back control and input direction, youll "warp" like maria & alucard
