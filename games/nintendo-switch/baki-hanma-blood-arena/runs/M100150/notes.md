**Baki Hanma Blood Arena**

*Survival, Demon Face*

*Basically, a Boss Rush*

*In-Game Time*

Each boss is different from the others: health, how long they stay dizzy, and several other details

Why version 2.0.4? Because version 1.0 is complete garbage. I can only fill the bar up to Lv. 1, the damage is also different, and it's even missing some voice lines

There is a mechanic in the game that I abuse with the TAS. For example, against Doppo, with the Charge Attack: if I hit him on frame 45247, he only takes damage. If I hit him on frame 45248/45249, he takes damage + 1 dizzy bar — whatever the fuck the name of this is, and if I hit him on frame 45250, he's already charging his attack
So basically, there is a 2-frame window where I can gain an extra dizzy bar immediately after the boss recovers from being dizzy

Body Shot and Jab fill the bar faster. They're also fast and deal more damage when combined into a combo

Some bosses require me to switch sides with Charge to hit them. Just press the button twice

Pickle is the hardest boss in the game. He's very fast

Yujiro has 2–3x more health than the others for some reason, which is pretty stupid. 102 hits just because I can

The basis for my SpeedRun is this video: https://youtu.be/T_HyVaoeHyo
