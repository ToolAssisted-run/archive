Platypus Reclayed is a side-scroller shoot 'em up. Like its original, Platypus (2003), its graphics are scanned and digitised models made of clay. Here is [one](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXjRGOqfDkg) of "making of" videos, although there a whole gallery of photos right in the game. 

Reclayed follows the ideas of the classic duology (although Platypus 2 is considered a non-canon part), but expands the whole gameplay and adds the actual ending. Some ideas got their improvements. For instance, since you collect fruits to fuel up your ship, why not shoot food at enemies? Yeah, that's hilarious, and still, it fits well into that surrealistic world.

Here is a good Wikia [article](https://claymatic.miraheze.org/wiki/Platypus_Reclayed) on the whole game, including its main gameplay elements (with sub-articles dedicated to power-ups, enemies, etc.).

## Game goals
The game is divided into areas. Each area is evaluated by ratios of destroyed major and minor enemies. 100% overall gives the biggest bonus score.\
Enemy sequences are predictable and time-based. For this reason, ~97% of TAS was done in slow motion.\
Area finishes once its enemy sequence ends (enemies are destroyed/vanished) and all fruits are collected or let fallen.

The rules are described in the category, but I'll repeat them here.
- Hardest difficulty
- Passive Resistance quest is passed.
- All other areas are passed for 100% evaluation.
- Deathless

## Other possible goals

# Gaining Score
In my opinion, maximum score is an incompatible goal, because there are infinite points generators in the game. For example, all level bosses shoot homing rockets you get points by shooting at. 
In this TAS, I tried to get big score by splitting and collecting fruits, as well as coins and extra lives. I also destroyed a few stars to gain more score if it doesn't affect time. And yes, I know that you magnetize fruits and coins easier, and I could have done better. 

# Fastest time
This one is a realistic goal, but it requires extensive planning. Especially, if you care about fast evaluations. Also, you deal damage by crashing into enemies.

# Pacifist
Also a realistic goals, but it's requires careful play, especially boss fights.

## Control

The control is simple. You move around the screen with your left analogue stick and shoot with a button (R). I'm a bit surprised the port didn't get joycon co-op mode, because it's pretty doable. The stick sensitivity is static. Maybe Nintendo Switch itself offers related settings.

## Difficulty choice

The game is relatively easy, so there is not much point in going for Easy difficulty. Nasty difficulty provides maximum enemies, they often shoot, their shot patterns are wider and minor ones shoot a projectile at you on destruction.

## Power ups

Through the game, you face special (groups of) enemies, which leave stars. Touching one gives you a weapon. Shooting the star makes it switch the weapon. Also, food ("hidden") weapons may be triggered by shooting a star until it switches to "?!" mark. Each start may have its own set of available weapons.
Stars can be destroyed if you shoot them for too long. In my TAS, I did it a couple of times if it didn't affect the overall time.

Platypus series has a rapid-firing trick when you quickly click Fire button. It works especially well on PC version where the framerate is unlocked.
In Platypus Reclayed, this trick was patched, but there is one weapon still sensitive to it: Fireball. When it's the end of an area, I use it.

## Level by level commentaries

Music arrangements go as follows:
- Level 1 and 2 boss fights use [Trap](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qcfFGpBi7ec) (Part 2) track, similar to the original Platypus.
- Level 3 music uses Ocean Loader 4 (default). Even though I like [Terra Cresta](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t7SjXvzLk0A), which was used in the game until a certain patch, I prefer the original atmosphere.

The hardest difficulty (Nasty) as well as Level 5 are unlocked by selecting "Unlock all content" option. I love this approach! No cheat-codes, no preliminary walkthroughs to unlock something. Just an option for everyone!

Also, it was possible to pick up weak weapons to show off TAS playaround, but since score gaining was my side-quest, powerful weapons were picked up instead. 

#### Level 1 Area 1 (Passive Resistance)

Passive Resistance "quest" is an option to pass the area without shooting any enemy and without dying. It takes about 30 secs longer than passing the area like normal, which can be watched [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNmFwyTlh1k).
Score-wise, collecting the crown (x2 score multiplier) is more beneficial than collecting another crown in the middle of Level 1 Area 4.

#### Level 1 Area 4

Since the crown was already possessed, gift boxes of destroying the factory building gives two extra lifes. Also, IIRC, this area is the only time-independent area where you can get beans weapon (a slightly worse version of bombs), but I forgot about it.

#### Level 1 Area 5

Skipping the caravan wagons doesn't save any time. I got lucky with enemy sequence, because it takes time to split and collect the fruits.

BTW, because Sonic Pulse weapon is broken in Platypus Classic mod, it's possible to destroy the level boss in just 3-4 shots.

#### Level 2 Area 5

Fortunately, enemy sequence is long enough to destroy all pieces of the boss.

#### Level 4 Area 5

The same thing. I could abuse fireballs to destroy the boss faster, but there would lead to a long flight doing nothing waiting for the remaining enemies to show up.

#### Level 5 Area 5

The final boss fight can be improved by a couple of seconds because its pincers cover its body at times. I think, Boxing Glove would be more efficient for phase 1. Also, I had to destroy some of its weapons because of intense fire.

### Possible improvements

- I took score gaining as my main side quest, and often I got really into it. Collecting fruits out of final area enemies could have been done faster, by... collecting them instead of splitting them for more score. I noticed it while about a half of game was passed, even though I knew from the beginning about evaluations showing up once all enemies destroyed/flown away and all fruits are collected, but I can't say which areas can be finished sooner. I think, about 40 secs can be shaved off.
- The final boss fight can be improved by a couple of seconds, as described above.
- Other improvements. It was difficult to make this TAS, despite slow motion efforts, because the Eden core and Chimera greenzone had their problems. I reported them, but maybe I missed something.
