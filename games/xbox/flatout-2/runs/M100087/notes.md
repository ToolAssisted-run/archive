This is a showcase tool-assisted speedrun of running xemu inside Chimera emulator. Xbox TASing is now possible and reproducible!

!! About TAS itself
Since console ports (PS2 and Xbox) run at 30 FPS, even in the main menu, you don't have much options of gaining score the way players do in PC version (beyond 600 points per an attempt). Just not much opportunities to do perfect skips unless your setup allows so.

The tactic of "aerobating" to the either side of the pool still works, but it's not clear how the game decides whether you can or can't do more skips. Sometimes it just disables the key even with enough energy still available. The practice shows that skipping gets disabled pretty quickly and there is no point in launching the driver a bit to the side, so you have more space while "aerobating" (ex., from the left wall all the way to the right wall).

Initially, I didn't plan to include the result of Attempt 2, but then I decided to keep it, considering that PC version records are unreachable anyway.

BTW, the game has RNG in it which propagates even into stunts. Copy-pasting input to get 3 identical attempts is not possible.
