!Introduction:
*Russian Affairs is an Adobe Flash 2D side scrolling shooter game created by Alexander Balanoff and released in 2004. It's most known for it's fluid graphics and animation for the time. There's not a whole lot to this game as it's quite repetitive with enemies and layouts. Also, getting a game over will intentionally take you to the credits, I've opted to play the full game.

!RNG/Optimization:
__RNG with Enemies:__
*The RNG on enemies can be effected in many ways such as flickering between different screens, changing the initial system time, and changing your movement. After releasing my ([UserFiles/Info/639120419158600146|WIP]), I thought that RNG was painfully difficult to deal with. I figured out after by accident that if you use your shooting animation for a single frame, every single enemy on that floor will miss you guarantee. Meaning, I don't have to stop and shoot enemies which is extremely slow to deal with.
__Cave Transitions and Keyboard Sequence:__
*These two are very simple. After entering a cave, It's faster to duck out of the cave as it skips the second cave animation which is way slower. Also, for a normal playthrough, It's intended that you wait at the keyboard area so that it unlocks the door to the final room. I wanted to test and see if I had to wait for it to unlock and it turns out, I don't have to. Visually, it's the same as the beginning of the run. But, the game detects that the door has been opened.
