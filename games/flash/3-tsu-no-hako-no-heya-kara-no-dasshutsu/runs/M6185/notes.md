3つの箱の部屋からの脱出 made by YominoKagura

Oh nice. A movie longer than 3 seconds.

__Routing:__

*Right, right
*Safe combination code: 038, get the screwdriver
*Drawer combination code: 291, get the pliers
*Left (We should see a picture frame in the middle telling us which order to press the buttons in those colored boxes)
*Go to yellow box, select the screwdriver, open the box and press the button. (The game forces us to pick up the key before pressing the button unfortunately)
*Go to the red box, Button combination: TL, BL, TL, BR, TL, TR. Press the button
*Go to the blue box, select the pliers, get rid of the ribbon, open the box and push the button
*Go back to yellow and blue and push those buttons in the following order
*Once you hear the door unlock, Left and leave the room
*Enjoy your lowercase "clear for the third time
