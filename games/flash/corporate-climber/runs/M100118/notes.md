Collecting all secrets and items requires 3 repetitions of the ladder:
1) Easy -- grab all secrets and items possible
2) Medium -- You give the green package to the receptionist and extract the wedding ring from the suitcase
3) Hard -- Give ring to the girl and get married -- then kill Satan

Plus 
4) Killing Satan unlocks Floor 13, which contains the last two secrets

I play through the end for entertainment. Some secrets need to be regrabbed to count (due to a naming bug in the game, and I do it anyway just because). Some secrets cannot be regrabbed in Hard because the coin escapes. Doesn't matter, I already got those secrets in easy.
