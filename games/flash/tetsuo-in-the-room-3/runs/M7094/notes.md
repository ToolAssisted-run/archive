!Introduction:
*Tetsuo in the Room 3 is yet another escape room game developed by Factory.112. I've decided that the other two games in this series ([UserFiles/Info/639020292203515743|Tetsuo in the Room], [UserFiles/Info/639023031428547372|Tetsuo in the Room 2]) were too short for a proper submission in my opinion. This is the final Flash title that I've TASed from this developer as I've enjoyed their style of escape rooms.
!Routing/Optimization:
__Tetsuo's Animation:__
*This game's optimizations mostly came from waiting for the animation to end for a specific action to continue moving. There was two areas where it didn't force me to wait which was the door opening transition after using the keycard, and repairing the time machine with the tape. Other than that, there was a lot of waiting.
__Text Boxes:__ 
*(This is practically the same solution when dealing with text from my [9685S|re-call] submission) Copying text beforehand and then pasting it instantly has been the fastest way for me to navigate this. Sadly, this game doesn't have the enter key shortcut that could save a frame.
