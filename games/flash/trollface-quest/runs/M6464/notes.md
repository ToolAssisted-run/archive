!Routing:
*Most of this game is trivial. So, I'll explain what's different below.

!Level 3:
*This is the first time we select something offscreen. The goal is to make it to the restroom. The men's room is locked and the game forces you to stay out of the female's room. The key kills you and so does urinating on the plant. By flipping the female triangle upside down, you can enter the restroom.
*Usually when there is an item on screen, there's a delay from the black screen transition. Performing the correct solution takes 2 mouse clicks with the transition delay. However, selecting the door offscreen takes one click with no delay. Basically, it does the solution instantly.
[https://i.postimg.cc/g01wNLKq/Screenshot-2025-02-26-101542.png]
