Escape from the Weapon Shop by Hottategoya

__Routing:__

*We immediately head to the top drawer and use the color code: G, R, B, G, G, B (Found via the colored spears and moving the second to top shelf box will reveal the order)
*Get the trapdoor handle
*Go to the bottom shelf and use the code: M, R, L, M, R, M (Found via the first open dagger showing the order)
*Get the key, head to the chest and get the wrench
*Head to the three boxes that are together and use the wrench on the screws. (Inside the box is a piece of paper revealing the code of the safe with the bottom half of the numbers missing)
*Go to the trap and use both the trapdoor handle and the screws to open the trapdoor
*Get the "concealed dagger" and head over to the small keyhole beside the large shelf
*The "concealed dagger" is a key that we can use on the keyhole thus revealing the safe
*The code from earlier is 8031. I unfortunately have to wait a few frames for the "open" text to disappear
*Get the key for the real door and I had to click three times to leave after selecting the key
