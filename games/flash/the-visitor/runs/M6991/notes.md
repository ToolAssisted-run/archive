!Introduction:
*Another classic flash title that I thought would fit for the spooky season. Anyways, "The Visitor" is a point-and-click horror Flash game created by Jay "Zeebarf" Ziebarth. The lore goes as you're a alien parasitic worm who's goal is to mostly consume anything living in it's path. This goes from small animals to as big as humans and you keep growing as you consume. There's about 3 games in total in this series, one being another point-and-click, the other being an open world type.

!Routing/Optimizing:
*There's a lot of cutscenes you can skip depending on how early you can select the next action (eg: the cat and the bird consuming scene) Other than that, order of operation and trivial stuff yet again.
