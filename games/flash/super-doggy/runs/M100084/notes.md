I am gonna tell my kids that this is Super Mario Bros.

The game is divided in four worlds with 3 levels each and they are played strictly linearly and always go left to right. Aside from the normal platforming stuff, the game has 4 distinct powers:
- Super Strength (Green)
- Super Breath (Purple)
- Flying (Blue)
- Laser Eyes (Blue)
- Super Speed (Yellow)

Each power is collected by either getting 20 small "🦴"  scattered across the level or getting a single big "🦴" that is placed in strategic locations. Purple and Red are strictly casual only. They look nice, but they stop the rightward speed so it's a no no. Green would be in the same category if not for the breakable boulders that strictly need Green to break them. Flying doesn't give extra speed, but it gives great flexibility. Super Speed is obviously very good, but given its in the last world, it's meaningfully used only once.

In terms of speed, there are few situations where you can get a different speed other than holding right:
- Strength / Breath / Laser Eyes - Makes doggy stop until the power is executed.
- Moving platforms - Moving in flow with them gives greater speed but moving against them slows down.
- The leaf high-jump thing in World 1 - Slows down briefly
- Taking damage knocks back, but doing it in a certain way we can make it a knock forward.
- Ice increases speed significantly 
- Super Speed

If someone looked hard enough, I am sure they can easily optimize this futher, but for me it was just a fun little thing. I think a high score or 100% category of them game would be fun as well.
