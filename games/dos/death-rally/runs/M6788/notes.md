!! Introduction

Death Rally is a mega charming racing game with plenty of violence. You get to buy and upgrade your cars and its weapons as you win races until you get to race "Adversary", your ultimate opponent. In this TAS, I mostly follow the pathing and techniques of the current [https://www.speedrun.com/death_rally/runs/z11pddwz|RTA WR] but crank the violence to the max and optimize the shopping choices. 

* Uses hardest difficulty
