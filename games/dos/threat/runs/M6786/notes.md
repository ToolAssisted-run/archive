!! Introduction

Threat is a 2D top-down Alien Breed-inspired shooter from Finland. It features extremely engaging gameplay, unsettling atmospheres, and a terrifying feeling of being in danger. I used to play this game coop with my brother and would be scared out of our minds trying to achieve each of the level's objectives. 

In this game, the levels are designed for the maximum (three) number of players. Nevertheless, they all can be solved by even a single player. In this movie, I play all three players simultaneously to finish Episode 1, going for the objectives right away even at the cost of taking damage or skipping monsters when possible.

In terms of strategy, this game has a shop where you can buy a diversity of weapons. These weapons are very useful to attack enemies that hide behind corners or when you need to deal AoE damage. However, I found using the initial Trigun and grenades led to the fastest solutions. The gun deals a lot of DPS and the grenades are useful for reaching enemies/targets beyond walls. So I didn't need to go for the fancier arsenal. It was a lot of fun to do this TAS. I will make movies for EP2 and EP3 in the following weeks, if I have time.
