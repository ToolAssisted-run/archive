An improvement over [M100105], thanks to Challenger's remarks about the tricks I used in my Gen version and forgot about.

* Level 8: you can just clip through the right (closed) gate in the ruined palace room
* Level 14: you need 2 fewer potions if you transform while dropping into the spider room
* Level 14: if you waste two hp and turn back to shadow, Jaffar will chase you, saving time. You drink a potion and kill him once he falls into the trap
