!! Introduction

Test Drive is a series of driving games that introduce the concept of car touring, as opposed to racing. Test Drive III: The Passion (1990) is also arguably the first open world driving game, where no boundaries are defined. Instead the (at the time) vast map circles around itself.

In this movie I finish the Pacific Coast - Yosemite course using the Corvette (fastest car) as fast as possible.
