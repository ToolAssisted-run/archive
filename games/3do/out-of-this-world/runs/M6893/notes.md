This version is based on the same VM and code as the DOS so many of the same tricks and skips work the same. Except that some exploits were polished out or outright fixed.

* Attacking with your feet during the Jail stage is removed. This helps accelerating the pick up of the gun.

* I could not find a way to have the friendly alien or myself escape through the gates on the Jail cell. So I had to wait for him to hack it up.

* You cannot hold gun charge through screen transition, so I couldn't insta-kill the two guards after you get rescued by the friendly alien
