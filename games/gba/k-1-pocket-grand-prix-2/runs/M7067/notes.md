The second game in the series is even more broken, now you can actually round1 ko with a super but only if you lower the enemy stamina fast enough. Because the enemy RNG gets worse, you need different to combos to account for the recovery of the enemy stamina bar. In this version we use the New Zealand char for more 100% combos.
