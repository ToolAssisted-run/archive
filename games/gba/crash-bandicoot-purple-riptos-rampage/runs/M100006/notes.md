Eight years before [M6737] I created a TAS of the completionist category of Crash Fusion, called [104%|https://www.speedrun.com/crashfusion?h=104&x=xn2y7z2o] , the goal of which is to obtain all 25 crystals, 25 gems and all 120 cards you can get with a single cartridge. I never submitted this to TASVideos because this isn't optimized to their standards -- for a more in-depth explanation as to why this is true refer to the submission notes of [M6737], specifically the section "The old TAS" -- but given this site exists now and because I think this is an effort worth preserving, I am hereby submitting this. Moreover, I will only post my old, unstructured run notes here because everything else is explained in detail under [M6737] as well as in the [https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cxWRRJaXxzIIRltD109Q9QZt6wJ4uo8B5OXx-HhCqsA/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.th3ttx1z5hr6|Sprash GBA knowledge base] by CDRomatron
