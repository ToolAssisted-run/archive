Digimon Racing is a kart racing game with its own spins in the genre and another attempt of mine to complete TASVideos with more variety of good obscure games. 

This TAS tackles the 3 Main Cups with Gomamon: Western, Eastern and Central. The other cups aren't played because they don't show off new stages or bosses, just use the same as before in a different order with no extra difficulty. The settings put the AI on Hard .

!! Game objectives

* Fastest completion of each stage and boss
* Takes damage to save time
* Uses RNG Manipulation  and precise skips to complete levels faster

!! Comments

Gomamon and his evolution have the best top speed and not so good handling, but TAS solves that issue.
Some laps were slower to manipulate not being hit by a Thunder strike, an item that hits you without any possibility of evasion.
There are a handful of stages in which some bigger skips were not possible because the game tracks your progress in the racing course in a way that doesn't allow for huge skips of the course's completion.

!! Special Meter

The meter does a bunch of different things simultaneously. When it's filled by a lot or when it's almost empty, it changes the digimon form and its top speed! When completely filled, the digimon can launch a special attack. Using regular items also consume the meter.

!! Stage by stage comments

! Bosses

The bosses have specific vulnerability periods and their fights are basically getting to an item as fast as possible to hit them as much as possible. The challenge is you still need special meter to use the item so you need to fill the meter a little bit before shooting. Also the stage layouts are varied and require some creative navigation.

The first stage allows for the double jump glitch but it was useless, not even jumping the pool was allowed by the game's progress check. A fun stage nonetheless.
