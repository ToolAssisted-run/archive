In the quest to complete TASVIDEOS and get more Digimon in the site... I've decided to TAS  Battle Spirits 2 which is based on the 4th Digimon Cartoon, i haven't watched it though.

Game is point based and is mostly an auto scroller until the final fight, which doesn't use the point system anymore and uses health instead.
I used HARD difficulty and default time to make it comparable to regular speedruns.

Kazemon is clearly the superior choice as her range of motion makes her more adaptable to reach the final boss spots to fight, she also can use some more suicidal strats with here air combo and digivolved special moves. Double jump, Fast Fall( down A in air), air combos, she's a handful!
Ledges and digivolution are both used to cancel moves delay.

In this movie i showcase a newly found semi infinite, let's see if you can spot it...

Beetlemon was tested but he's much slower, even though he's fast, and Lobomon was considered before i realized how horrible his air moves are, the other digimon are even worse.

I didn't test the unlockable Loweemon or Ex forms but that's another category altogether.
