Apple Cider Spider is a short platformer. It features three levels and seven difficulties. In this TAS the seventh (six) difficulty is used.   

!! Game objectives

* Emulator used: BizHawk 1.11.1

!! Comments

*There are no shortcuts in this game!
*Jumping is the fastest form of movement.
*Collecting numbers causes the spider to stop for a moment. It can't be avoided.
*Between levels there's a huge delay on the seventh difficulty which can't be avoided either.
*The direction the spider is looking after climbing up a rope depends on the side the spider is climbing.
*Turning on ropes is generally slower. Except when waiting for an object to pass, or depending on the distance to the next rope.
*It's not possible to jump under low ceilings.
*There's lots of waiting in this game.
*In higher difficulties the game runs faster and there are more objects.
