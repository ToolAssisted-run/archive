Kelly Kangaroo is a homebrew vertical platformer released by Denebola Interactive.

Kelly the kangaroo is enjoying a lovely picnic date with Rosie the rabbit in the picturesque Mossy Valley when - Oh no! The mad scientist Contemptus Mundy, always bitter and jealous of the happy people living on Gaia Gaia Island, has kidnapped Rosie and locked her away in his motherbase in the clouds! It's up to you to help Kelly save her sweetheart before it's too late and that vile Contemptus whisks her away to the stars.

Tags:

- Emulator used: BizHawk 2.8

- Genre: Platformer

About the TAS:

For this run, I used the original "0.3" release of the game since it's faster than the newer version. This TAS only syncs on 0.3.

The main trick I used was wall jumping. By going into a wall before touching the ground on top of it, it's possible to jump in mid-air.

The transition from the ladder section is very specific. You have to be at a pixel-perfect position.

Input ends when I jump to land on the final enemy that bounces me into the final screen.
