''Circus'' is a 1983 text adventure published by Digital Fantasia, part of the ''Mysterious Adventures'' series, that received ports to many other systems. In this game, your car runs out of gas, and you find yourself in the middle of nowhere. That's when you stumble upon a mysterious circus, and the secrets keep piling up...

This run was made on the 128K model since it boots the fastest. The inputs were based on a solution from [https://solutionarchive.com/file/id%2C112/|CASA]. After the game loads, pressing enter removes the pictures, which saves a lot of time. Words can be shortened to 4 letters when applicable.
