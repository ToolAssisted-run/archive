Another Prince of Persia version! This version was developed and published by Riverhillsoft in 1992.

!!__Game objectives__

* Emulator used: Bizhawk 1.11.9

* Aims for in-game time instead of real time

* Takes damage to save time

* Abuses programing errors in the game

* Sega CD BIOS: us_scd1_9210.bin 


!!__Why did I choose the Sega CD version?__

* The gameplay's mechanics is the same as the PC (DOS) version, which includes several bugs that also work on the Sega CD version. In addition, this port includes:
 
* A more detailed intro, with cutscenes and voices.

* In-game timer (like the SNES version).

* A speed adjustment option on gameplay and on battles (the default speed gameplay is 3 while speed battle is 4).

* An extra Jaffar battle is included next to the Princess's room before you finish the mission.


!!__Level-by-level comments__

__General note:__ Almost all of the tricks/glitches used in this game require good positioning, in order to work these tricks/glitches.


* Level 1 - No comments.

* Level 2 - Thanks to a good pattern optimization of the fourth guard and a good pixel position, I did an extra jump to save time without wasting time to avoid the spike trap.

* Level 3 - Getting hit from the skeleton saves a little time.

* Level 4 - It's possible to force the guard to open the gate without needing to take the usual route to open the required gate to progress, saving a bunch of time.

* Level 5 - I attract the guard in order to abuse a glitch to bypass a closed gate without requiring to climb up some floors then go to the left and land back to another point from the first room, saving a lot of time.

* Level 6 - The shortest level of this game! And skipped a boss.

* Level 7 - A glitch is abused falling down in a wall to skip a third of the entire level. 

* Level 8 - This level is hard on many occasions and the longest one of the TAS, as well as the fact that if you fail close to the end, you have to start from the beginning again. At least the latest gate after you open the exit door is avoidable if you return just in time.

* Level 9 - Another long level. I strike the first and the last guard to avoid slowdown on the following room, respectively.

* Level 10 - Two guards are killed while planning to perform two glitches, respectively. The first glitch is grabbing on a ledge at the bottom edge of the screen at the exact time when entering it. Using the glitch, another instance of skipping one third of the same level to gain another benefit.

It works because the game assumes that you grabbed the ledge from the screen below and moves you up instead of down.

The second glitch is clipping into a closed gate, which requires getting hit by a falling tile and turning around at the right timing while positioned next to the gate. Killing the guard was required for safety reasons.

After the second glitch, I did a trick during the exit room to avoid the stun animation when landing directly to a wall, saving time.

* Level 11 - No comments.

* Level 12 -  Climbing down and back up again puts you a pixel further to the left, which is enough to let you safely knock the tile down, skipping a few rooms when playing this level normally.

That glitch used after the first guard on level 10 is used again to reach the Prince's reflection path with the sword still present there (normally it disappears when you reach this room). However, the reflection won’t appear until you pick up the sword, enter the next room then immediately return to the actual room, fixing the issue. 

In the DOS version the target spawns without problem, but for some reason it doesn't work properly in the SegaCD port - he spawns between the bottom and the top of the screen - merging the reflection with the player will send you one floor below instead of following the bridge. In order to prevent this bug, hitting the target at least once fixes the problem for sure.

Once the “Jaffar” battle starts, I force the Boss to go next to the pit and win the battle with only one attack! After this, I reach the exit door (this is the only door that’s possible to access the stairs before it opens fully).

One last section remaining, time for a final battle with Jaffar! Yes, this version has a final battle before rescuing the Princess.

Input ends before Prince does a final hit on Jaffar.
