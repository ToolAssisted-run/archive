!!!Oils Well
You're rich!...maybe. Geologists have just located one of the largest oil deposits in North America, on your land! But you have to get it out of the ground before you can spend it.

The other oil barons are jealous, and they would do anything to sabotage your operation. They have planted oozies and land mines in your oil fields to wreck your drilling equipment.

Don't let them cheat you out of your millions!

More information can be found in the [https://www.gamesdatabase.org/Media/SYSTEM/Commodore_64/manual/Formated/Oil-s_Well_-_1983_-_Sierra_On-Line.pdf|manual].

!!Effort In TASing
I've done a lot of games in the past, where routing is everything. In this one, routing is even more important...due to the horrendous dangers that need to be avoided. In addition, each level has a number of concepts that needed to be taken in consideration:

*The Drill Bit
**Cannot directly consume "Land Mines".
**Must devour every "Oil Pellet" to complete a round
**Frequently must destroy the "Oozies", head on.
**Uses the "Petromine" to slow down all moving obstacles on screen to allow more time.
*The Drill Pipeline
**Cannot be touched by "Oozies"
**Will allow passage of the "Land Mines" and "Goblets"


!!Ending Choice
This game has a very clear ending, which rewards the Oil Baron a "Stock Certificate" . Afterwards, it starts all the way back at the beginning on a second loop.
