!!!BC's Quest For Tires (Commodore 64)

!!!Tools Used
*BizHawk 2.9.0

!!!Effort In TASing
Because a version existed for the Colecovision, I wanted to get the C64 done. This version is less exciting, as it lack speed mostly. Other mechanical differences exist though..listed below.
*Jumping (side to side movement) was locked to the screen scrolling, unlike the Colecovision where there is about half of that speed (better in my opinion). 
*Air hang time, is shorter. Colecovision is much longer and gives better obstacle avoidance.
*Acceleration was extremely slow. Colecovision is once again the faster running game and more worth a TAS.
