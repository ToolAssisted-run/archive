!!!Word Match (Compute's Gazette)
This is the classic word matching game where you select two squares to match what is hidden beneath.

The article for this game can be found on page 80 of [https://archive.org/details/1983-10-computegazette/page/n81/mode/2up|Compute's Gazette Issue 4 (October 1983)]

!!Why TAS This Game?
The continuation of TASing games from my all-time favorite magazine, Compute's Gazette. This makes my 104th TAS from this series. 

Never played this game, because this kind of stuff never excited me back then. Since I'm trying to complete the library for this magazine, I have to get through this one...as it is not all that appealing. Many pardons, but look out soon...a big one is coming!!!

!!Game Difficulty and Ending
There is no difficulty selection; however, this game has a clear and definite ending....where you are notified of your victory. The only caveaut, a human would have to make perfect selections, otherwise you forfeit your turn to the computer...who has the potential to "Run the Table".

!!Effort In TASing (Not BOTed)
There is nothing to this one. Just match something, and if it wasn't correct...go back and fix it.
