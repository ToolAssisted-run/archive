The evil witch Magica De Spell has stolen Scrooge McDuck's Number One Dime and kidnapped his nephews, Huey, Dewey, and Louie. Donald Duck embarks on a mission, traveling around the world to save his three nephews and recover Scrooge's lucky dime.

!!__Game Objectives__

* Emulator used: Bizhawk 1.11.4 / 1.11.7 (18:33 v2 TAS)

* Takes damage to save time

* Abuses programing errors

* Manipulates luck (To get hammer weapon, not frizbee or diamond)

It's 1 minute & 36 seconds (96,7 seconds or 5176 frames) faster than ShinyDoofy's run.


This TAS of this game introduce some Tricks:


__Jump Technique__ 

As well as Ghouls'n Ghosts (Genesis & Arcade Versions), jumping is faster than walking! 
It's not exactly a trick, but saves time! 


__Ledge Glitch__ 

After defeating a Boss of Stage 1, 2 or 4, if Donald there balancing on a ledge at right side, instead to go of center screen, Donald will 
at right corner of the screen, saving much time, depending of position.
