This game is a great platform fighter, it's a little outdated but features Digimons from Seasons 1 to 3 Anime series.

The goal is to beat 1 player mode as fast as possible on Hard. This is different from speedrun, because we have no characters unlocked and i get challenged by black wargreymon, which is only avoidable by losing a match (completely 2 rounds) or by having unlockables. BWG is like Akuma challenge when you play well.

Renamon and Sakuyamon are recommended for both their high damage and versatile combos. Renamon has a big projectile which decimates big body enemies, but isn't as strong with small chars. Renamon has good damage though, is fast and if the stage allows for a lot of sequential abuses of the stage drops is the faster choice. Sakuyamon has a good super move and infinites.
In the first round, i do some slower combos that avoid multiple digivolutions. Sometimes, i manipulate enemies into being into the air so they don't digivolve but on HARD difficulty this is very rare. When round 2 starts, I can't avoid the digivolution but at least i kept it to one per match.

I couldn't manipulate the minigame at the half of the TAS, there surely is a faster one but I don't know how.
