!Category Rules
* Beat MetalGreymon on BIOS Domain. 
* Timing starts on selection of "New Game" and ends at the "Win"-message after the last hit on MetalGreymon.

!Strategy
* The main strategy was to use paralysis to make enemies miss without having to wait for the perfect RNG.
* Floor layouts were also manipulated for optimal traversing through domains which impacts not only the time spent but the need for digibeetle upgrades.

! Defeat vs Recruit
* The TAS was actually for an older version of the same category called "Recruit MetalGreymon" which played exact the same but you had to recruit him at the end.
* The only real difference in the new one is that you don't need to give him the gift prior to the fight.
* Despite the TAS itself being for an older version, the timer shown on the video is based on the newer ruleset.
