!! Introduction

This game is the official Argentinian version of PC Fútbol, a series of superb soccer management games. Here you select a team in the "Primera División" (Premier League) and try to win both local leagues (Apertura and Clausura), as well as the Copa Libertadores, the South American analogue of the UEFA Champions League.

In this movie, I choose my childhood team, River Plate, and play for an entire year, winning all games and clutching all three trophies. Note that the only thing missing would be to win the Intercontinental Cup (a single match featuring the winner of the Libertadores vs the Champions league's), but that would require playing through an entire additional year, and I just didn't find it worth it.

For extra (yet unnecessary) mischief, I scoop Diego Maradona (yes, THAT Maradona) out of my arch-rival team, Boca Juniors. I even eventually manipulate the game to have him score against his former team. Had this happened in real life, it would have been the scandal of the century. 

The strategy consists in changing tactics and players to manipulate the outcome of the match. The delay from the moment you make a decision and its effect on the match is pretty large, so some impending events (goals, red cards) are barely avoidable.
