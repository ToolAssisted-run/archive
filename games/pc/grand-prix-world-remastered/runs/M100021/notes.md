!! Introduction

Grand Prix World is one of the, if not the, best F1 management simulation. It hits the perfect balance between realism and depth, and intuitiveness and a not-so-steep learning curve. "Remastered" is a community mod that was designed to avoid the most frequent crashes, fix some of the driver's names (originally blocked by licensing issues), and other quality of life issues. I chose this version mostly, though, because it comes with an otherwise un-emulable CD protection mechanism.

The game is inherently hard -- I used to play this back in the day, with never much success. I discover today that was because of the teams I chose; always wanting to win with the underdogs. Turns out, playing with Minardi, Steward or any of the other is impossibly hard, that is, if you don't know exactly what to do. Minardi, as it stands, represents the hardest challenge -- with an extremely limited initial budget, a failing team, and a slow-as-snails cars. 

Playing with Minardi represents a completely different challenge to that of teams like McLaren or Ferrari, and requires a totally different (unintuitive) approach. Whereas with the former, you would want to improve your car and go for the points immediately, lesser teams fight for a different goal. Not for the championship, but for SURVIVAL. Once you learn that, the game transforms entirely, providing an entirely different challenge. This is what gives gives the game it's depth and makes it an absolute hidden gem.

In this movie, I tackle the Minardi challenge head-on, changing history, and achieving the ultimate success in just 5 seasons (out of the maximum 10 the game plays for). I do this by completely abandoning the competitive aspect initially, and concentrating on the team's financial success until we can think about racing again. The strategy, which I explain in detail below, comes from my own experience, but also from thoroughly studying several sources for weeks before starting this work:

* [https://gpmplanet.proboards.com/thread/104/gpw-hints-tips-long|Comprehensive strategy guide] by alfikhoazka. This guide solidified in me the idea that the approach with low teams is to first guarantee survival, not points. 
* CaptainGoodSpeed's entire [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLsVZ1tbHyX9AjaMHcDm45YavobEzlTO4a|Sauber let's play] playlist. Gave me a lot of new clues, mechanisms and tricks I didn't know about. The most valuable, which I completely ignored: the car's chassis handling is the most important base stat contributing to a car's performance.
* DefinitelyZero's [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL0kpXBjT2UE9MtuaQ2R-p6lOXxKx-8cHN|Let's Minardi playlist], which although misses many of the tricks I use in this movie, gets the gist of the strategy right and help me make some decisions.

!! Strategy

The movie requires 5 seasons to complete the goal. I believe 4 seasons might be possible, but the level of luck and foresight required would be so high that it's unfeasible. It would require, for example, testing many approaches whose success can only be determined until the last season (making this a multi-year project). Instead I went for the safest-of-the-wildest approaches, never getting too close to absolute (nigh or far) disaster. The strategy changes drastically through the seasons. Nevertheless, here are two tricks, which I developed myself, that I employ throughout the seasons and are key to success:

* Develop driving aids, which are maintained through the seasons, and will allow help towards the performance in the final season. Don't care about FIA approval; we won't be caught cheating anyway if we dedicate enough staff to car security.
* The most important trick in the first seasons, and my own discovery: if you manipulate RNG to allow the current year's chassis to be legal into the next year, you don't have to build new cars at the end of the season, which is so expensive you'd have to take a loan for it, ruining yourself financially. I chain this manipulation through the seasons, only building cars in the last one, where I effectively have infinite money (loan which I won't pay)

! Season 1 (1998)

The goal is absolute financial survival. The main and immediate goals here are:

* Hire the two highest paying drivers: Diniz and Takagi -- they will pay so much that will be the main source of income for the team. We need to hire them for several seasons until we're ready to switch to more skilled drivers for the last (winning) season.
* Hire one competent commercial manager with the least Royalty %. This would be E. Manzoni, which has 2 ability and 0% royalty.
* Hire the absolute cheapest managers for the rest. We don't need any design work, and the minimal amount of mechanics/engineers suffices to keep two minimally qualifying cars
* Look for the cheapest Engine, Fuel and Tyre deals, and then focus all commercial work on cash sponsors. One of them will take the place of team sponsor (duplicating money), because team sponsors are completely unavailable at the beginning.
* Start building the factory to allow more staff in and have more design facilities towards the last season.
The whole season focuses on getting these deals and qualifying minimally to the races. This is done by not caring at all for the cars' wear levels, but only keeping them under 15% damage. Unfortunately, the game adds 5% damage to the cars when they are worn, so at least we need to use 2 spares every 3 races. However, by switching one of the cars with the third car, that helps us keep things running a bit more.

! Season 2 (1999)

This season is a repeat of season 1, but with much more emphasis in getting better deals.
* Shotgun (10% to each sponsor) to get a hefty and long-lasting team deal (>20M$)
* Keep increasing the commercial staff and building up the factory facilities further
* To support finances, I sell 10% of my stake on the company

! Season 3 (2000)

This season keeps giving emphasis to commercial success, however:

* Start building up the design team, so that next year we can start designing the ultimate car
* Hire the best possible design chief (5-star) which is the most crucial aspect in designing a winning car. Without this, it's not possible to win races consistently.
* Build all the design facilities (CAD, CAM, supercomputer, wind tunnel). To finance this, I sell 39% of my stakes -- enough to retain 51%

! Season 4 (2001)

The last push -- here we make sure we get the best technical (engine, fuel, tyre) deals towards next year.

* Hire the best driver we can (Mika Hakkinen), preserving Pedro Diniz as second, for financing
* Start designing the winning car for 2002.
* Get the works deals for engine and tyre (fuel unfortunately I couldn't manip my way to it). Also get a great deal on a team sponsor, although this is not strictly necessary due to the Loan abuse (see next season).
* Start building up other teams (mechanics, engineers), which will be crucial to keep the team running during the winning season

! Season 5 (2002)

This is the season where our team wins all races.

* Enable all driving aids, even if illegal (CHEATER!!), because we will dedicate so many staff to security, we'll never get found out.
* Use testing to improve engine and types, but more importantly, our chassis handling.
* Cars become so damaged at this level (I don't know why, I suspect it's the use of illegal aids), that repairing and maintaining is impossible, even if I had the best team. Luckily, the sollution for this is to build entirely new cars altogether.
* To finance the extreme expenses of this season, I take a Loan I never intend to repay (lol).
* RNG manip by slight car setup changes is a must to win races.
* The final races I don't even care to fix the cars, as it's the last push to the TAS finishing.

At the end of season 5, I reach the goal: winning the constructor's championship and 1-2 in the driver championship, having won all races.

! What can be improved

I think a 4-season run might be possible, but this would be razor thin. The most important factor is to find a 5-star designer at the end of season 2, so we can start designing the race winning car during season 3. The finances would be extremely tight, but a good Loan trick at the end, getting all the money possible might help with the last push.
Beyond that, I realize mouse movement is not extensively optimized. I wouldn't have  been able to enjoy the process (and thus finish this move), had I had to manip mouse frame-by-frame. There are many subtle pauses in the game that are annoying to predict. So I made this movie as an impossibly fast yet still human user.
