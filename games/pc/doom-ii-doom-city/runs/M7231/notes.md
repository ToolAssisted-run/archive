Doom City is a custom Doom wad, which consists of a single map. Map 2 loads the standard Doom 2 map. %%%
This map brings custom graphics to give the map a more realistic look, which feels more relatable than official Doom 1/2 city-style maps.
It can be downloaded from [https://www.doomworld.com/idgames/levels/doom2/d-f/doomcity|idgames] or [https://dsdarchive.com/wads/doomcity|dsdarchive].

!! Game objectives

* Emulator used: XDRE 2.21 (based on PrBoom+ 2.5.1.4)
* Doom goal: UV-Max (complete each map as fast as possible on Ultra-Violence difficulty while killing all initially placed monsters and revealing all secrets)
* Contains speed/entertainment tradeoffs
* Takes damage to save time
* Aims on in-game time instead of real time
* Heavy luck manipulation

!! Comments

! Demo playback
* Make a shortcut of dsda-doom.exe or prboom-plus.exe
* Open the Properties of the shortcut
* Insert commands at the end of its Target field, so it would look similar to that:
%%SRC_EMBED 
D:\dsda-doom-0.25.3\dsda-doom.exe -iwad doom2.wad -file DOOMCITY.wad -playdemo cityx117.lmp 
%%END_EMBED

! General comments

In March 2022, due to a dramatic situation I had to go through, I only had my enterprise MacBook Pro with me. I installed VirtualBox, so I could build Doom TASes. It took me 4 days to get prepared because some software refused to work due to low technical graphical limits. My conditions were really artisanal: 
# Windows 7 was maximally optimised for weak hardware.
# Only Doom Builder 1 managed to launch (GZDoom Builder Bugfix wasn't showing some thing placements).
# XDRE was lagging.
# I couldn't watch my demo with OpenGL render to see alive monsters and utilise walkcam, so I had to listen to all monsters by their IDs and notate alive ones.

I was surprised most things worked as planned. It takes much time to grab the SSG, so I decided to ignore it. The only time it would come in handy is when I was finishing the pain elemental (thing 75).
Some time was lost on the shotgunner that didn't wake up when I was passing by. 
Ammo management was perfect. No excessive ammo.
