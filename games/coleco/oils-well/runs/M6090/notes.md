!!!Oil's Well (Colecovision)
You're rich...maybe. Geologists have just located one of the largest oil deposts in North America, on your land! But you have to get it out of the ground before you can spend it.
The other oil barons are jealous, and they would do anything to sabotage your operation.

More information can be found in the  [http://www.colecovision.dk/PDF/Oil%27s%20Well%20-%20CV%20-%20Manual.pdf|manual].

!!Effort In TASing
When I did the Coimmodore 64 game, last year, I started to create the Colecovision version. I don't know why I didn't look earlier, but I finally decided to check if a TAS already existed for this game. Well, I found one and I quickly stopped after I saw the name [user:BrunoVisnadi] on it. It has been my experience with any TAS from [user:BrunoVisnadi], that no frame is left behind. Well, just recently, I was watching the existing TAS and saw that a movement was not being done, as in my C64 version. I decided to check it out and was able to find 8 frames off of the first level. This prompted me to going through the entire game, as I saw the ability to make cuts.
