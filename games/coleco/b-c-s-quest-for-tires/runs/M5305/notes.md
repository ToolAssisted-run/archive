!!!BC's Quest For Tires (Colecovision)
This is a 2 frame improvement over [5808S].

!!!Tools Used
*BizHawk 2.9.0

!!!Effort In TASing
I have been working on the C64 version and wondered if I had learned anything that could be used on the Colecovision version. So, I started TASing the coleco game, while avoiding the inputs from [user:MESHUGGAH]'s [5808S]...as I didn't want any influence to take away from my understanding thus far. Well, it turned out [user:MESHUGGAH]'s inputs were rock solid! The only thing that showed up was a difference found when jumping the top of the volcano, by use of the bird. Something in the cut scene causes a timing issue (Frame Rule maybe?) that would soon be resolved by the end of Stage 6. Thereafter, each stage was 2 frames faster and that is how it ended...with a 2 frame cut. All I had to do, was hold that ground until the game was finished. 

I applaud [user:MESSHUGGAH] and his efforts!

!!Bizhawk 2.2.1 Confirmation
