!! Introduction

This is the official Prince of Persia Amiga port. The movie is an adaptation of [5206M] where the biggest difference is a guard RNG behavior. This change was enough to prevent me from botting the game using [https://github.com/SergioMartin86/jaffarPlus|JaffarPlus], having to replicate the routing of my DOS movie manually. Overall I think I resolved the execution quite nicely.

Another difference is that this game being cracked doesn't have the copyright room that I also used in my DOS movie to manipulate RNG. However that means this movie doesn't tradeoff RTA for IGT. It satisfies both goals simultaneously.
