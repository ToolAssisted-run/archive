!! Introduction

Super Casino 2 is a gambling game for the SNES. It offers several machine/card games to gamble on and the goal is to reach 1 million dollars as fast as possible.

The game starts in the low max bet lobby and allows you to get into the higher max bet rooms as you gain more money. When you reach 200k, you are invited into the high roller lounge, where you can bet even more.

In this movie, I play only single numbers at the roulette (the fastest way to make money) and abuse luck to win always. I also abuse RNG by waiting a few frames to have the lucky numbers be those closest to the starting position of the hand.

There was no need to enter the high roller lounge, as I only need to advance to the second lounge to get 1 mil.
