DinoCity (1992), is a platform video game developed and published by Irem Corporation for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System.

The game centers on Timmy and his friend Jamie, two young children who are accidentally transported to a world resembling prehistoric Earth populated by anthropomorphic dinosaurs. While a device which will allow them to return home exists in this environment, a critical component has been stolen by Mr. Big, leader of a gang of Neanderthals known as The Rockeys, leaving the player to guide Timmy or Jamie to his castle to retrieve it. They are assisted by Rex, a Tyrannosaurus, and Tops, a Protoceratops, each with their own play-styles.

!!__Gameplay__

Players are given the choice of controlling Rex with Timmy or Tops with Jamie at the beginning of the game as well as at the start of each new level. While Tops can throw darts to damage enemies from a distance, Rex is confined to close combat by punching them or swinging his tail. Each dinosaur character can defeat enemies by jumping on them, and at any point the player may relegate control to their human riders, who can freeze enemies in place by using a special energy device. While controlling the children, the dinosaurs become immobile and may be used as platforms themselves to reach otherwise inaccessible items.

In order to pass each of the game's six levels, players must navigate through a number of screens joined by doors. Each screen resembles a different environment, and can include caves, jungles, grasslands, or ice caverns. Once a player has passed every 5 screens in each stage, they must battle a boss at the end of each stage. 

To start the game, I choose Tops because he can throw darts to hit enemies from a distance, and that helps to reduce lag in several points of the game even when a “single screen” is very laggy.

!!__About the screens and doors__

* There are 6 screens in each stage and there are two doors (red door and yellow door) at the end of every first 4 screens. 

* On screen 5 and screen 6, only the red door appears.

After beating 2 screens (no matter which door and route you have chosen), every screen 3 and 4, the red door leads to the bonus stage - not required for this run at all.


!!__Tricks and Glitches__

__Floating/Hover trick__

All you need to do is jump, and while in midair press R every other frame. This trick was originally discovered by Blackpearl back from 2006.

__Rapid Fire trick__

In order to perform this trick, you should press R for one frame, press Y in the next frame, then press R in the following frame and repeat the instruction as many times as possible.

If you want to auto-fire with only one attack type, here’s the command:

Press R, wait 1 frame, press R again, press Y, repeat the command, and so on. Trick discovered by me.

__Perfect landing trick__

If you land on the edge of a platform, it's possible to walk in the air until you get up completely. It's very useful in several ways. This trick was found by Rawr.

__Invincibility cancel boss glitch (only applies to Boss 2, 4 and 6)__

If you fire a dino attack alongside the human freeze/ripple attack and they touch one of these bosses at the same time, although they can’t be frozen, for some reason it prevents the stun+invincibility state so instead of waiting for the next cycle and more, they can be killed in no time. 

This unusual glitch was discovered by me.

__Deadly Freeze-Jump attack glitch (it applies to Boss 1, 3 and 5)__

The freeze attack adds an “extra effect” not normally noticeable until you test out of the blue: while being frozen, you can jump then touch on them at the same time their HP depletes very quickly - it is actually used only on the boss 3 because this idea is slightly faster than autofiring him. 

That was discovered by Mecha_Richter.

__Wall Jump glitch__

To perform this glitch, you need to be pushed a little to get inside of a wall, allowing you to jump inside.

It's possible to use the dino attack while doing each jump to boost extra height. Glitch discovered by me.


!!__Stage-by-Stage comments__

__General notes:__


* Most of the game uses the floating trick, which saves an insane amount of time, usually.

* This game lags so much that certain locations barely attempt some action, such as the “circus” autoscroller sections.

* Once upon starting every ice cavern section, it's faster to do a jump then press R twice to gain normal speed instantly rather than just start walking.


!!__Stage 1__

* Near the end of the forest path, on the last set of 3 worms, if you let the top middle one eat you, it will take the player to the ice cavern as if you entered the red door, for some reason. Saves some time.


__Boss: Crasher__

* The strategy: Uses the rapid fire trick with a bunch of darts to destroy the blocks while he's frozen barely after the battle starts.

!!__Stage 2__

* On the first screen, after reaching the top, because I jumped on a specific position (the camera hasn't scrolled up), one of the enemies didn't spawn, avoiding some lag.

* On the third screen, with good precise positioning and autofiring the R button with an extra frame of delay, I can bypass these 2 skulls smoothly.


__Boss: Monster Mole__ (x2)

* This is the first of three bosses that the “ripple attack” doesn’t work as intended. As I mentioned before about the invincibility cancel glitch, if you get both projectiles to hit the target at the same time, not only does the boss stun animation fail to work but also allows a great opportunity to defeat both moles with more than enough time in a single cycle!

* It's possible (with Rex) to defeat them without the glitch because his attack works very quickly.


!!__Stage 3__

* Autoscrollers aren't an easy chore when TASing: you actually want to add some good entertainment value but the lag is quite difficult to prevent.

__Boss: Trampo Bird__

* Freezing the boss then jumping on him with the dinosaur, it damages 1 HP each frame, defeating him almost instantly and even faster than the rapid fire trick.

!!__Stage 4__

* On the ice cavern path, the wall jump glitch is used right after the start. It's a bit faster than using the blue platform boost.

__Boss: Pole Press__

* This is the second of three bosses that can be defeated faster with the weird and unusual “freeze glitch”. Great idea!

* His stun time lasts longer enough that the glitch destroys the target in a blink of an eye.


!!__Stage 5__

* During the “circus” path, I intentionally take damage from the moving spikes just to avoid lag, if things were already worse than before.


__Boss: Fire Bird__ (x2)

* Mecha Richter told me that it's possible to use the same deadly glitch used on the third boss of this game, but the rapid fire strategy got more advantage with these dart projectiles.


!!__Stage 6__

* As soon as the first section starts, it's possible to drop the dinosaur for quick boost positioning while waiting for the required wall to progress.

* Near the end of the vertical screen before the last boss, a wall jump is done to skip an elevator, followed by a floating trick at the top, saving a good amount of time.


__Boss: Mr. Big__

* Normally, you need several cycles to defeat this boss. Also, this is the last boss to include the same strategy from the second and fourth boss. This time, it actually does some effect by damaging him with the darts while the boss shrinks through the ripple effect.

* However, because of his teleport pattern, it isn't possible to finish the fight in one cycle.
