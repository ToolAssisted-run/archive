!! Tricks used in this TAS

* Backwards jumping

To perfom backwards jumps press left one frame before the charakter is able to jump. But it don't work after throwing a boomerang or a hammer.

* High-jump cancel

After a High-jump it's possible to cancel the jump by throwing a boomerang or a hammer. Usefull in vertical levels.

* Backwards running

At fullspeed press Right+Left for a few frames, this will cause the charakter turns around, accerlate by pressing Right, after several frames press Right+Left again and so on. This is slower than normal running, so it's only for entertainment.
