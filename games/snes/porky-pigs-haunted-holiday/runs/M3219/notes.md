Porky Pig's Haunted Holiday is a 1995 side-scrolling platform video game developed by Phoenix Interactive Entertainment and published by Sunsoft for the SNES. The goal of the game is to guide the main character, Porky Pig from the Warner Bros. cartoons, through his nightmares. 

The story goes as follows: On the night before Porky Pig's vacation starts, he goes to bed. Asleep, he starts having nightmares where he meets different scary characters. In order to wake up, he must travel through all of the levels in the game.

Notable graphical effects include parallax scrolling and a weather effect which means that every time the game is played the weather can be different. For example: sometimes it may be snowing and sometimes it may be raining.

__Tricks__

__Moonwalk__

To perform this trick, press left+right. It's funny, but saves time in one part of Stage 1.


__Slope Jumping__ (Name and some information stolen from Castle of Illusion SMS version TAS)

During sliding, if you jump without pressing Left or Right for some frames, you can exceed the speed-cap for a short time.


__The Camera Trick__

In several parts of this game, I perform this trick in order to manipulate enemies position (such as spiky grass in one path of Stage 2 and several goos of Stage 4).


__Bypass through the walls__

When Porky Pig reaches the ceiling of a wall while still standing on the platform that rises (such as bubbles on Ruins of Atlantis in Stage 3), If you hold the 'X' button, Porky Pig enters a wall. If you drop 'X' Button several frames after entering a wall, Porky Pig will travel upwards very quickly at the cost of taking damage during a zip.

The glitch was originally discovered by StarBoy91 (by accident), but Challenger (myself) improved this glitch.

__Stage 1 - The Haunted Woods__

__Stage 2 - Dry Gulch Town__

* Weather: Morning (Sun).

Implemented several slides in this Stage.

During the entire Yosemite Sam boss sequence, before he shoots bullets, I duck sometimes to avoid some lags caused by his bullets.

__Stage 3 - Atlantis__

* Weather: Cloudy and a little dark.

In the first section, unfortunately, due to having 3 hearts instead of 4 (because you're into hard difficulty), I need to wait more seconds than intended before moving forward to the end.

In the third path, thanks to 25 cupcakes collected, I gained 1 more heart energy. Thanks to a glitch, I reached more than half of this path much faster than before.

In the boss fight, it's possible to hit the enemy twice only for the first cycle while the remaining cycles allow only one hit.


__Stage 4 - The Abandoned Mines__

Used camera trick several times to manipulate these enemy goos in order to avoid damage.

In the path before the boss, I press the jump button 15-16 frames every time to climb the chains much faster.

__Stage 5 - The Alps__

* Weather: Fog.

It's not possible to manipulate RNG position of ice spike enemies, losing some frames to avoid a damage (One of this hearts is necessary to save time in the next bunch of of ice spikes)

To finish the autoscroller path, you must reach a certain point where this path ends. That's why I jumped on 2 birds near the end of the section.

A lot of time is saved skipping several doors of Wacky Land parts (The middle of this Stage 5).

This Boss (The Yeti) of this stage is the only boss that's possible to hit 2 times each cycle.

__Stage 6 - The Castle__

Input ends before the last hit on the robot.
