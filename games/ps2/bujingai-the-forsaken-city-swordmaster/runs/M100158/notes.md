You also have to be very specific with how much you hit him because if you do a combo on him, he will fly out of it. That's why we miss the first hit to not do as much damage on him. So i will try my best to explain as much as possible starting from when the fight starts: 

1. After you skip the cutscene that shows Lau spawning on the platform you have exactly 1 frame (9 frames after you skip cutscene) to do any input. So what we do is hold R1 (AKA: target) and X to jump forward which is why we spawn so close to the boss.
2. Try and make the boss attack early (Hopefully earlier than i could) and jump to get his attack to whiff. Afterwards switch the spell to Senranken lv 3 (Wind  Spell) and make sure he gets hit 7 times. 
3. Afterwards you wanna switch to the Tekkuken spell (Stronger Attacks) and run up to where he is about to fall.
4. Make sure to whiff the first attack to not do too much damage to him (He will fly out of the combo if you do too much) and combo with 1 attack cycle.
5. Cancel your attack into triangle (I have remapped it to R2 for personal use) and swap to Dankuuken Spell (Sword Flip attack) make sure to get all 4 hits and you should win the fight.
