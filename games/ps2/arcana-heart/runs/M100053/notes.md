Arcana Heart is a 2D fighting game for the PS2 released in 2007.  

The strategy here is choose Fiona similar to the current WR here : https://www.speedrun.com/arcana_heart/runs/zxwe41em

Fiona is hard hitting and slow, but this works in our favor. The game severely curtails damage for consecutive hits in a combo so a character that does really high damage in short combos works out to be fast.

This is the USA version and one difference I noticed is that the versus screen, where it shows portraits for both opponents, for the Japanese version seems slightly faster.  If there is a way to make that faster for the USA version I do not know it.

For this Arcade Mode opponents gradually scale up in health and difficulty.  Once the mirror match is complete the final round is over.  

I ended input on the final Results before the Credits.  There are some additional inputs in the Credits version of the movie in the additional files, but those do not need to be included for the movie.
