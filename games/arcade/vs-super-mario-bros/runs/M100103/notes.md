A new TAS that took a month and a half to complete. This is the world-record TAS of Vs. Super Mario Bros. (with warps).

Vs. Super Mario Bros. is an official arcade game released in 1986. It shares many similarities with the original Super Mario Bros., while adding more challenging levels.

This TAS saves 67 frames over Zen's previous TAS (on a different platform): 2 frames in 1-2, 1 frame in 4-2, 63 frames in 6-1, and 1 frame in 7-4.

The ground clip at the end of 6-1 is really special. It relies on a special wall-clipping glitch at the very bottom of the screen, which I first discovered in 2021 (video: 1-1 Full Flagpole Glitch with Ground Clip).

Authors: HappyLee & Asumeh
