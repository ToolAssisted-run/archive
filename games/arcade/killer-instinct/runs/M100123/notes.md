This is a showcase TAS featuring Cinder. The goal of this run is to demonstrate high-level character mastery through a mix of unique glitches, bugs, massive unbreakable combos, and simple dynamic sequences to keep the gameplay engaging and fast-paced. You might notice the absence of the "Shadow Combo". This was intentional, as performing it requires an active Combo Breaker (having executed at least one Combo Breaker during the match).
