**Informations:**
- Game: Street Fighter II' Turbo: Hyper Fighting (World 921209) 
- Difficulty Level: 8 (Hardest) 
- Character: Guile

**Mechanics:**
- What makes Street Fighter 2 CPS1 series iconic is the ability to chain combos while canceling low kicks into punches most known as CPS1 Chain, the technnique is very simple: press both down ,low kick + any punch when you are standing and low kick + punch whenever you are crouching at the first frame the game animation allow you to connect another low kick, the same thing works for when you want to cancel either consecutive punches/kicks into jump action just press up/up right/up left, low punch + low kick, for jumping you need to specific press both low kick + low punch so you can end a combo with air kick/punch jump canceling animation.
     
**Movie Preview:** 
- Guile is probably by far the best character for a showcase since World Warrior, this run demonstrate how many combo resources a charge fighter can pull through chain combos on the first turbo game of the series.
