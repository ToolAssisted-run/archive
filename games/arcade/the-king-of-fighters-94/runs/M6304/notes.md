Hardest settings tool assisted speedrun!

I use Andy bogard, the fastest known damage dealer, other strong combo characters use more hits for the same damage, but as in most old games, damage is somewhat variable, all fights have trades to avoid perfect which is very long and usually doesn't cost a lot to get hit and lose that message. You cannot edit your team in KOF94 so I had to use team italy in its full composition, but to be fair there aren't more relevant characters in any other team anyways.

Andy has  loops in the corner which help to control the necessary damage to end the match, he can actually infinite combo in the corner even though the meter doesn't count hits.The infinite isn't faster but since i wasn't able to open Rugal guard a lot, it was a necessary choice, i did another version without infinite and it was actually slower because Rugal blocks so much.

If you want to watch the original speedrun that gave us these strats, watch https://www.speedrun.com/pt-BR/the_king_of_fighters_94
