I managed to get my bizhawk working again and to celebrate i TASed the Heidern strategy for KOF94.

Heidern moon blade has much more damage when counter hitting and even more with MAX gauge filled. Heidern can also whiff  a moon blade to enhance the damage of a command grab SEVERELY, one of his glitches(the grab takes the damage from the moon blade), and also whiff a command grab to cancel a normal move as long as the command grab is slightly off distance and doesn't connect.

Unfortunately, command grab infinites are very slow and not useful for the tool assisted speedrun.

With these glitches, you can see how different is the HP for each enemy. I tried using a super to finish Rugal but it didn't cause enough damage so i just used a command grab to finish the fight.
