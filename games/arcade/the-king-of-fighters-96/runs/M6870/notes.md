This movie uses infinite combos that don't stun and takes damage to avoid Perfect message, it's a very small improvement over an existing great TAS by R D D. In the last round of each fight, i don't need to take a hit though since there's no message.

Sometimes it's better not to infinite to get a counter hit and finish the fight trading blows.
