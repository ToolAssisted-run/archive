More SNK games!

I got interested on this game for reading about a special mechanic that allows us to skip a round, the ultimate KO, but the problem with this UKO mechanic is that you need to lose a lot of health fast to use a super move WHILE your enemy has less than 20% health himself. That means it was better just to find a fast damage character. 

I decided to use Sinclair which is normally a sub-boss in this game but she's immediately available for playing on Arcade for no good reason. Despite being a sub-boss, because of her slow strong damaging attacks, she's considered one of the Worst Char in the game. Character can wiggle to get out of the ground and avoid OTG moves but for Sinclair i got decent setups for all my moves. Characters all have different weight classes and behaviors so i can't spam the exact same combo for all. The last combo should be your favorite probably! Being on air and Counter hits help with more damage, so would red health but i never get hit.
