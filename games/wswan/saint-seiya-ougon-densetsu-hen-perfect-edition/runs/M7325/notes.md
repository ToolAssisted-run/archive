!! Introduction

Saint Seiya: Ougon Densetsu Hen Perfect Edition is a WonderSwan remake of a combination of the two Saint Seiya games for the NES (mostly the first one), both of which I TASed:

* [5473M]
* [5438M]

This games features much better graphics, but the gameplay isn't better than any of the above. A lot of the fights were removed and there is an extreme amount of dialogue to replace them. Some things don't make sense either, e.g., there is a stat for jumping that you can spend EXP on, but the player cannot jump!

For walkthrough, I followed mostly this [https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/wsc/918343-saint-seiya-ougon-densetsuhen-perfect-edition/faqs/74522|guide] (Spanish). As per glitches, there aren't any. Only RNG exploitation to maximize attack power and decrease/dodge damage. I also transfer all life to cosmos before I visit Miho, to maximize cosmos.

I figure this is It's a nice TAS to watch if you know the anime. You won't understand anything, otherwise. But yeah, now I have my first WonderSwan submission and something to showcase at [https://www.dbcade.com/asf|Anime Speedruns Festival] next year.
