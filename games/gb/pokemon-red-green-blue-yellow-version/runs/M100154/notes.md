__Summary:__ 

A ~4 second (243 frame) improvement on the previous known record, swapping out the early-game Pikachu encounter with a Rattata on Route 2 and keeping battle animations turned on to save roughly 2 seconds overall by the Brock fight, plus broader state space search giving frame-level micro optimizations throughout the rest of the run to pick up another ~2 seconds of time save by the hall of fame.

Credit to MrWint for the original route pathing plan, and to gifvex for the Route 2 encounter change and battle animation switch idea: https://tasvideos.org/Forum/Topics/22668?CurrentPage=1&Highlight=506457#506457

__Time Improvements:__

By targeting a defense DV 0 squirtle at the start of the run and encountering a Rattata in route 2 instead of a Pikachu, this saves 59 frames by the end of the fight. As tackle is also faster with battle animations on, we also save time here by deferring disabling it until later inside Viridian forest, where we no longer need tackle and switch to using bubble - this also saves 104 more frames against the previous record, additional input search luck included. The rest of the run's frame saves come from a wider search over each state space for trainer fight RNG and movement.
