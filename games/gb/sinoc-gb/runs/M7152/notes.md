!!Techniques used

!Lag reduction

*Jumping:
Jumping can sometimes cause 1f of lag, so its good to avoid jumping when that will happen. 

*Badnicks:
The badnicks cause a imense amount of lag on the game so the ideal situation is to have as few as possible on screen, however killing them at the first possible frame can either get extra lag by jumping or get you on a bad positioning. I just tried jumping on a lot of different places to see which one was the fastest, while still giving me a good position.

!Spring ignore

Normally on Sonic games you go up as soon as you touch a spring, but here this isnt the case. Touching it will do nothing unless Sonic is going down, so i use this to have better control of his position.

!!Level by level breakdown

!Level 1

This is basically a sumarry of what i just said with better lag reduction than last time, though if you have good eyes you probably noticed that i didn't do a glitch i discovered to go under the level. Basically there is a badnick up there and if i go under the level there is no way of killing it, so i discovered that going up and killing the badnick is barely 2f faster with perfect movement.

!Level 2

Most of the same, mosty just better lag reduction, but at the end i do another trick. Sonic deaccelarates only after touch the ground so i jump before beating the game so Sonic takes more time to lose all his speed and allows me to finish the movie earlier, saving around 20f.
