gameboy game based on dodgeball anime

Supershot everyone and then try to minimize leftover ball to the enemy team in this TAS, i also noticed you can sometimes shoot enemies out of sight and this gives you control of them which would be useful for RTA but for TAS doesn't seem so relevant, this starts happening at the end of the TAS on the tournament section. Some enemy formations are very bad and i prefer shooting the second guy on the line of sight so i don't necessarily drop the ball. The first enemies would pass the ball to me but the other almost always shot me so i avoided letting have the ball.
