''Idk but it's something trying to mimic Super Mario Bros in a unique way!''

!! Game objectives
*No damage taken
*Luck manipulation
*Fastest time

!! Comments
*This game is heavily inspired by Super Mario Bros. Tokoro moves like Mario, you can't go back to the left and there are few layouts similar to SMB. The gameplay is very simple where you just move and shoot enemies with a water gun.
*You also have a map that shows you the path you're going through, though the first four areas are chosen at random among some others (except the starting area), so this kind of created a bit of randomness that requires some manipulation but also choosing the fastest path (choosing Minato for instance is not guaranteed going from the first area).
*Other than that, it's just walk, jump and shoot type of game.
