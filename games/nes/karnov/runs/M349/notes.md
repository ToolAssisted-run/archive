Only a few seconds of this improvement are because of routing changes; the improvement is almost entirely because of faster gameplay/tactics.

*Level 2: 4 timeunits faster
*Level 3: 1 timeunit faster
*Level 4: 7 timeunits faster
*Level 5: 6 timeunits faster
*Level 6: 2 timeunits faster
*Level 7: 11 timeunits faster
*Level 8: 4 timeunits faster
*Level 9: 13 timeunits faster (doesn't include the final boss fight, which is also considerably faster)
