!! Introduction

Pinball is one of the first games for the NES. In this movie, I programmed [https://github.com/SergioMartin86/jaffarPlus|JaffarPlus] to find the quickest way to reach the bonus stage, where you get a chance to rescue Pauline (our damsel in distress) for a hefty bonus. I used JaffarPlus once again there to find the solution with the earliest last input that results in rescuing Pauline.

This movie is almost half as fast as the current [https://www.speedrun.com/pinball/runs/ywvl6p0z|RTA WR] by [https://www.speedrun.com/users/ShesChardcore|ShesChardcore]

Enjoy!
