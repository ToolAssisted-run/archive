''Let's go for some treasure hunting!''

!!! DuckTales 2 "best ending" in 13:19.999

!! Game Objectives
*Takes damage to save time
*Luck manipulation
*Abuse death to avoid backtrack
*Best ending
*Difficult mode

!! Comments
*So this game caught my eyes a while ago, and noticed that the best ending version of this game was not done yet. I decided to make one for this category, though due to my irl stuff (studies and work), I took it a bit slowly. It wasn't that complicated to understand and make, but it was challenging a bit especially with RNG manipulation
*I made use of [2424M|current Any% run] by feos and MESHUGGAH. Their run is fantastic and it was always a reference to return to for tricks and details.
*RNG manipulation seemed horrible at the start, as the first boss REFUSED to give me the pattern I want that's similar to Any% run, with all possible adjustments I could think of, so I had to give up around 11 frames to not good kill height. Thankfully, other bosses were either free of RNG manipulation or easier to handle.
*This game has a big lag problem, which is quite difficult to handle properly. It becomes even worse after purchasing extra two HP slots which are necessary to obtain the remaining treasure map piece. I decided to delay the purchase until before Scotland area so the lag isn't increased in earlier areas.
*Oh, there is also a 4 seconds improvement to Any% in MU. Instead of going all the way to the end of the first screen, you can just get the item that let's pull heavy objects then fall down after to take a quick shortcut. It can be seen at [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PUwJPzNSa-o&t=393s|6:33] in the above encode. (''EDIT: Turns out it's not possible as it requires pogo upgrade from Bermuda area. There is a slight chance it's still faster though, but by a marginal amount of frames.'')
*All in all, this was a fun little project to work on. I felt it would be cool to showcase this category as well and revive this game (Any% run is from 2013 which is around 13 years ago now.. ''Time flies!'')
