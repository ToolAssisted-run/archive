In January 2004, the first ever TAS submission (at NESVideos, later called TASVideos) was my initial attempt at NES Ghosts ‘n Goblins. Now 22.75 years later, I’m submitting my 12th attempt.

This movie is 12 frames faster than the previous version.

Stage 1 (9 frames saved): I saved 6 frames by changing some actions in the graveyard segment. This change in timing caused the Red Arremer to behave differently for the damage boost, but ultimately I preserved the earlier 6-frame gain. The boss fight also changed because of the timing difference, leading to another 3-frame gain.

Stage 2 (3 frames saved): Historically, 6 frames are intentionally conceded in the early part of this stage to avoid spawning the first Big Guy enemy in a bad position. However, it’s possible to navigate around the Big Guy while giving back only 3 frames before the bird timer activates, for a net gain of 3 frames. The “double damage boost” in the house is not faster.

Stage 3 (0 frames saved): I could not improve the enemy gauntlet at the beginning of the stage. I also could not improve the boss fight.

Stage 4 (0 frames saved): I looked into the potential out-of-bounds unintended warp that was initially discussed 11 years ago. While it has “theoretical” potential for a warp, I do not see a way to use it in practice. There is otherwise little potential to save time on this stage.

Stage 5 (0 frames saved): There does not seem to be a realistic way to improve the wait for the platform. I could not improve the boss fight.

Stage 6 (0 frames saved): I do the first damage-boost section 3 frames faster. This gain can be maintained through the second damage-boost section despite the change in timing. However the boss pattern is determined by timing. I have to delay 21 frames to get the best pattern. (The delay previously was 18 frames without the 3-frame improvement.) I would need to improve the earlier part by another 21 frames to get the best boss pattern. The patterns with less delay take much longer to beat. I believe the boss fight in the best pattern is already optimal.

Stage 7 (0 frames saved): I do not think the final boss can be improved.
