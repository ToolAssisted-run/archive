!! Comments

There is no major route change since the previous TAS, such as which rooms to visit or which items to collect. This 37f improvement over [6300M] instead consists of many tiny time saves (room numbers refer to the ones used in the pyramid map link above):
* 22f from better negotiating platforms during vertical climbs (7-1 (2), 7-5 (3), 7-9 (2nd visit, 2), 15-E, 16-1 (1+1+4), 17-1 (2nd visit, 1+4), Princess' room (2) and Belzebut's room). The hitboxes are a bit unintuitive in this game and haven't been analyzed in detail.
* 9f from optimizing fall speed to reach a floor or platform faster or collecting an item earlier (1-1, 6-E, 10-1, 11-E, 14-E (2), King's room (2) and Princess' room)
* 1f from better block breaking (Princess' room)
* 2f from minor trajectory changes to increase fall speed, resulting in reaching the exit faster (7-7 and 7-8)
* 2f from "obvious" input mistake (moving further horizontally than needed, 13-E and Princess' room)
* 1f from eliminating random lag (15-E)
(* I used this review to also polish some of the inputs to make some sections look slightly more interesting, at least in my eyes.)
