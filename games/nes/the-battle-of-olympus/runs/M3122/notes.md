This TAS improves [590M] by __2302 frames__.

There were only 3 people on the team that made this amazing game. Yukio Horimoto intentionally [http://www.usgamer.net/articles/legend-of-love-the-making-of-the-battle-of-olympus|reproduced the gameplay style of Zelda II]. Like Zelda II, the game has a [Forum/Posts/426982|superspeed glitch when you use L+R]. In The Battle of Olympus, you have to build up maximum right speed before you can switch to maximum left speed.

Before I started this TAS, I made my goal 09:59.99 or better, even though I had thought it was impossible. The final time of 10:01.43 gives me motivation for another attempt at some point in the future. Right now, I need time to psychologically recover from olive collecting.

This TAS was a solo project, but [user:nesrocks] deserves legacy authorship credit for creating the route and some techniques. The table below tells you exactly how I improved compared to the currently published movie.
