__About the Game:__

Pinky Xmas is a 2020 NES homebrew made by Axbakk Games. You play as Pinky the flamingo, who has to traverse through 5 levels and defeat a candy cane and evil Santa Claus to save Christmas.

__About the TAS:__

This is a 55 frame improvement over my own current publication thanks to falling faster on ledges and a new strategy from [user:nymx]. It was originally a 58 frame improvement but 3 of those were lost due to RNG.

See my previous submission of this game for the level explanations.

Thanks for reading.
