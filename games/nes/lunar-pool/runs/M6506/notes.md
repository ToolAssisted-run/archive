I SWEAR I AM DONE NOW.

Ok, in my previous movies there were people both here and in youtube asking for a max friction movie. I guess they thought I wouldn't pay attention, but I just cannot escape a challenge. Also squeeze the last drop of juice from my bespoke bot.

So. Max friction. It's a different challenge because many more strikes are necessary to score all balls and each one of them becomes more fine-grained. 

Since the exploration space is much larger in this case, I had to adopt some additional heuristics to make it work. The most important of them being: use only the most powerful shot (and just in case allow for one or two lighter ones).
