Batman: The Dark Knight is a RomHack of Batman (NES). It has new level design and color palette, this is slighty less difficult than the original game.

Game Version: 2.9.6

The level design actually allowed me to do smooth movement and entertainment stuff at various locations, this TAS aims for fastest time.

The fun shortcut performed in 3-2 saves time in seconds, I figured it during TAS making.

In 4-3 and 5-1 Pause glitch was used to avoid damage.
