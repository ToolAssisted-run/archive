Rush'n Attack improved by 233 frames. It obsoletes a 
Famtasia movie.

Usually, when a character lands after falling, he pauses 
for 1 frame. I discovered that if a character attacks 
15 frames before hitting the ground, he will continue to 
run when he lands. There are also some tactical 
improvements, and lag has been reduced.

Notes:%%%
If a character touches a ladder, he automatically grabs it. 
'Regular' enemies are part of boss battles (most obvious 
on stage 4). On stage 5, it is faster to climb the ladder 
at the beginning part, because of a screen-scrolling 
problem. On stage 6, it is faster to climb the ladder than 
to die on the mine.
