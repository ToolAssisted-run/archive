!! Introduction

Ninja Gaiden 2 is a game with ninjas and swords. In this run, we eschew the latter.

Category Rules:
* Hurting enemies is forbidden during normal stages and only permitted during boss battles.
* No scenery or bonus orb can be hit either.
* Taking damage to progress faster is allowed

! Previous Work

Xipo had submitted a [https://tasvideos.org/1423S|pacifist, damageless] run for the japanese version that reached the end in 12:10. In my interpretation of the ruling, the choice of damageless introduced many long waiting times that made the movie a bit akward. More recently, [https://gong-lue.com/|Gong Lue] has a new [https://pan.baidu.com/s/14QsxmrXQhn0qtd76NEKUqg?pwd=6666#list/path=%2Fup|pacifist, damageless movie] that reaches the end in 10:58. He, however, allows himself to destroy orbs and gather Ninja Power and weapons that he later uses against the bosses. Comparing my work against either of these movies is apples vs oranges, but they serve as the closest comparison point. 

On the other hand, I find it strange nobody (that I know of) even tried to go for a pacifist% of this game. It is, in principle, a simpler game than its predecesor.

! Strategy

Just like in my [https://tasvideos.org/4812M|Ninja Gaiden I pacifist movie] the main problem towards the later levels is HP management. Using damage as boost is very useful, but Ryu is limited to 16 HP and each enemy hit removes at least one. So one needs to plan the route very well and know where it is worth it to introduce short waits instead of getting hit. After level two, I manually routed all levels to finish with 1 HP, maximizing speed. After routing this, I programmed the bot to balance speed and damage skips as much as possible.
